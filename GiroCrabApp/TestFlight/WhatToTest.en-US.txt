GiroCrab iOS 0.7.5 (Build 24) – includes the complete GiroCrab v0.8.7 feature set:

• New banks: BHW Bausparkasse (including mailbox PDFs) and Kontist business accounts (official GraphQL API), plus updated 1822direkt FinTS endpoint.
• Transfers & booking list: Manage/hide known recipients, tap the new note indicator on bookings to jump straight to the note editor, automatic focus in the TAN prompt, and FinTS transfer fixes (immediate execution date & HISPAS pain.001.001.09 detection).
• Improvements: Preserved VR casing and legal-form stripping in merchant names, prevented short-acronym false matches without counterparty, supplemental foreign-currency totals, and iCloud sync for account notes.
